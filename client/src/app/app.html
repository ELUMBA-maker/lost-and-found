Why we built this

The space between lost and found.

lost&found exists to close the gap between losing something and getting it back.

Every year, thousands of items :phones, keys, wallets, backpacks go missing on campuses, in offices, and around neighborhoods, not because they're truly gone, but because the person who found them has no way to reach the person who lost them. Homeward fixes that gap.

We built a simple, structured way to report what's lost, log what's found, and let your community do the rest matching people to their belongings faster than a bulletin board ever could.

No middlemen. No lost cause. Just your community, connected.
